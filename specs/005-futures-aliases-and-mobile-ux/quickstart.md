# Quickstart: B3 Futures, Shorthand Aliases & Mobile Navigation

Quick reference for working with newly added futures aliases, commodity names, and mobile features.

---

## 1. Using Shorthand Ticker Aliases

You do not need to memorize complex symbology. Type the alias directly in the "Selecionar Ativo" input:

| What you want | Shorthand you can type | Canonical Ticker | Formatted Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini Índice B3 | `WIN` | `^BVSP` | `pts` (e.g. `208.500 pts`) |
| Mini Dólar B3 | `WDO` | `USDBRL=X` | `R$` (4 decimals, e.g. `R$ 5.0235`) |
| Ouro Spot / Futuro | `XAUUSD` or `GOLD` | `GC=F` | `$` (e.g. `$ 4,172.00`) |

---

## 2. Navigating on Mobile Smartphones

1. Open the application on your smartphone browser.
2. In the **Terminal** tab, tap **Filtro das 48 Estratégias** to expand the strategy drawer.
3. Swipe up or down anywhere on the screen (even directly over checkboxes or accordion headers). The page scrolls smoothly without getting trapped.
4. Select individual strategies to overlay buy/sell arrows on the chart.

---

## 3. Finding Champion Strategy Combinations

1. Navigate to the **Simulador** tab.
2. Enter your asset ticker (e.g. `PETR4.SA`, `WIN`, `WDO`, or `AAPL`).
3. Click **Otimizar Combinações**.
4. The engine evaluates historical buy/sell strategy pairings and displays the Top 5 configurations ranked by net profit and win rate.
5. Click **Aplicar Campeã** to immediately load the winning configuration into your active simulation parameters.
